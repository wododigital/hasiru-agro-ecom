## Assumptions
- A13-1 (2026-10-20): aliases are a `text[]` column on `places_district` (BUILD-PLAN section 3 line for `places_district` has it); no `places_district_alias` table. Search runs in memory over the 645 rows.
- A13-2: HQ positions are one-off OpenStreetMap Nominatim lookups committed as `data/seed/district-hq.json` (ODbL attribution in the file). 466 are a headquarters town, 179 are the district centre (flagged in `review_notes`); distance is therefore approximate for those. Nursery origin for haversine is Bengaluru city centre (12.9716, 77.5946) until the nursery's own coordinates are in settings. Distances are rounded to 5 km; the 12 HANDOFF samples override (Hubballi sample 410 not used, Dharwad 430 is).
- A13-3: extra states and territories (12) are added to `places_state` so every pincode has a state and a GST code; the sheet only covers 24.
- A13-4: Karnataka plus Vijayanagara (31) default to `page_enabled`. `products.json` was not there yet, so the seed could not count fitting plants; the 6+ rule is enforced at render (noindex and left out of the district sitemap below 6 fitting plants) and `districts.ts` will apply it at seed time as soon as `data/seed/products.json` exists.
- A13-5: fit thresholds beyond the research text (rain below 40% of the minimum = not recommended unless drought tolerant; high tolerance softens down to 70%; above 2x maximum = not recommended; humidity no overlap drops good to ok) are mine, in `FIT_RULES`, documented in the README.
- A13-6: the sheet's Kanpur Dehat temperature maximum is 99 C; it takes Kanpur Nagar's temperatures, flagged in `review_notes`. District names renamed to current official ones beyond the plan's list: Davanagere, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Dharashiv, Ahilyanagar, Prayagraj, Ayodhya, Sribhumi, Thoothukudi, Tiruchirappalli and similar, all keeping the sheet spelling as an alias. Em/en dashes in sheet text were replaced.
- A13-7: Andhra Pradesh keeps the sheet's 13 legacy districts, so 489 AP pincodes and 70 Telangana pincodes (newer districts) have no district; they still have a state. District is left blank rather than guessed.
- A13-8: the pincode directory is the CC0 mirror `harsha1205/Pincode-India-CSV` of the India Post / data.gov.in file, downloaded to `data/raw/pincodes/india-pincode-database.csv` (23 MB, not committed because `.gitignore` does not cover it; see request 4). The step re-downloads it when absent.
- A13-9: image sourcing is Wikimedia Commons by botanical name through `source-missing-images.ts` (deterministic scoring, a reviewed `REJECTED_FILES` list with reasons after I looked at contact sheets of every photo, `EXTRA_QUERIES` for retries). Magnific stock was searched for the banana rows and Ehretia but nothing was downloaded: stock files cost credits and carry the stock licence rather than CC, and the results were unrelated or no better. The 65 masters were recompressed once to 1400 px JPEG (the log hashes are of the stored files).
- A13-10: photo quality is mixed on purpose and honest: some are flowers, leaves or fruit rather than a whole tree (see `subject` in the log). Weak ones to replace with nursery photos when the client has them: banana (Yelakki, shared with Robusta and Red; a leaf photo, not cultivar specific), Monkey jack (fruit pile), Alangium, Butea monosperma, Cassia fistula, Capparis moonii, Ehretia is missing, Baraba is missing (no botanical name; client to confirm the species).
- A13-11: the district page uses default `placesMessages` (a package route cannot read the app's `messages/en.ts`); components accept a `messages` prop so the app can pass overrides. JSON-LD on the page is a BreadcrumbList only; the seo module can add more.
- A13-12: no route stubs were written under `apps/shop/app/...`; they are generated from the manifest by `modules:sync`.
