## Assumptions (numbered A07-n, dated)
- A07-1 (2026-10-20) `inventory_level` carries a denormalised `product_id` (not null) and a `sku` snapshot. Why: the `stock.changed` / `stock.low` payloads need productId, and the product tab and PDP section must list a product's variants, but another module's tables may not be queried and catalog has no server API yet. Set by seed, admin count and ERP mapping.
- A07-2 (2026-10-20) `inventory_reservation` has pk `(id, variant_id)` and a `reference text` column instead of `cart_id` / `order_id`. Why: the frozen inventory-ops contract returns one reservationId per multi-line reserve call and takes a free `reference` (payment attempt or order id).
- A07-3 (2026-10-20) `inventory_level` adds `erp_on_hand`: the last ERP number, recorded even while a manual override holds. Drift data for the report and the value `clearOverride` restores.
- A07-4 (2026-10-20) Manual override triggers: `setLevel` (admin count) and `adjust` with reason `adjust`. `adjust` with reason `cancel` (restock of a cancelled order), `updateLevelSettings` (threshold, ready days) and order commits keep the source.
- A07-5 (2026-10-20) `commit` on a released reservation throws `ReservationError('released')`; `release` on a committed or released reservation is a no-op. Restocking a paid order that is later cancelled is `adjust({ reason: 'cancel' })` by orders.
- A07-6 (2026-10-20) `setFromErp` treats the ERP number as the new `on_hand` (clamped at 0), updates only existing levels (unknown variant ids are returned in `missing`) and skips items whose `updatedAt` is older than the stored `erp_updated_at`. Orders committed here but not yet known to the ERP can be "added back" by a pull until the outbox delivers them; WP-27 should push orders before pulling or pass `updatedAt`.
- A07-7 (2026-10-20) Commit floors `on_hand` and `reserved` at 0 (an ERP pull may have lowered stock below a hold); the ledger records the actual change.
- A07-8 (2026-10-20) `stock.changed` is emitted on every change of sellable stock, including reserve and release, so PDP "only N left" stays fresh; `stock.low` only on the downward crossing of the threshold. `seedLevels` and `updateLevelSettings` emit nothing (the latter still expires the stock cache tags).
- A07-9 (2026-10-20) Back-in-stock delivery has no custom job: inventory subscribes to its own `stock.changed` and sends when sellable > 0. The sender comes from `setNotificationsProvider()` (same pattern as core `setAuthProvider`); unbound means subscriptions stay pending. Template vars passed: `productId`, `variantId`.
- A07-10 (2026-10-20) Notify-me consent: the explicit request plus the required `consent: true` checkbox; `inventory_subscription.consent_id` stays null until customers exposes a consent API (A28). No lead is created yet (no service to do it).
- A07-11 (2026-10-20) `/api/notify-me` uses honeypot + rate limit (10/h per hashed IP), not the form time trap: the PDP is prerendered, so a time token would be older than 24 h.
- A07-12 (2026-10-20) Settings: `defaultLowStockThreshold` (5) and `notifyMeEnabled` (true). The research default "threshold = bulk_min_qty" is applied by the seed data (`low_stock_threshold` per variant), not by code.
- A07-13 (2026-10-20) Preorder purchases are not reservable (reserve needs sellable stock); a preorder flow is out of scope.
- A07-14 (2026-10-20) Nothing written under `apps/shop/app/`: the owned paths `(admin)/admin/inventory/` and `api/notify-me/` are produced by `modules:sync` from the manifest (verified with `planFiles` in `manifest.test.ts`).
- A07-15 (2026-10-20) Job `inventory.release-expired` uses policy `singleton`, retryLimit 2, expireInSec 50, and loops until a pass releases nothing (500 rows per pass).
